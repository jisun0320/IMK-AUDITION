A message from
Sonja Steindl

Sonja Steindl
Intendant, IMK · Initiator of IMK Audition

Dear Young Musicians,

Music has been at the heart of my life for as long as I can remember.

Since moving to Vienna in 1972, I have had the privilege of living in a city where classical music is not only a great tradition, but a living part of everyday life. For the past 33 years, through IMK, I have devoted myself to bringing music to the stage and creating opportunities for artists and audiences to meet.

Over the years, I have encountered many exceptionally gifted young musicians, including scholarship students from the University of Music and Performing Arts Vienna (mdw) and the Kronberg Academy. Their dedication and extraordinary talent have deeply impressed me, but they have also reminded me of something essential: talent needs opportunity.

For a young musician, there is an enormous difference between studying a concerto and performing it with a professional orchestra in an internationally renowned concert hall. Such an experience can become an important step toward a professional career.

I came to believe that those of us who have spent our lives in classical music have a responsibility to create more of these opportunities for the next generation.

This conviction led me to initiate the IMK Audition—our first project dedicated to discovering outstanding young musicians and giving them opportunities to perform with professional orchestras on distinguished international stages.

To every young musician considering applying: believe in the work you have done, have the courage to share your artistry, and give yourself the opportunity to be heard.

I sincerely hope that IMK Audition will open new doors for talented musicians and help the next generation take meaningful steps onto the professional concert stage.

I look forward to hearing your music and seeing where your musical journey will lead you.

Sonja Steindl, Intendant, IMK

About IMK

IMK — Verein für Internationale Musik- und Kulturförderung — was founded in Vienna in 1992 and has become one of Europe's most active classical concert organisations, putting international artists on many of the world's great stages.

Today IMK connects and organises approximately 200 performances each year at renowned concert halls across Europe. As a professional classical concert management agency, IMK partners with music universities around the world, including the Kronberg Academy and the mdw – University of Music and Performing Arts Vienna.

Why orchestral opportunities matter

Solo recitals and chamber music concerts are relatively easy to organise. However, for emerging musicians, even many such performances may not be enough to attract wider attention. Orchestral performances are different: they require a professional orchestra, a large concert hall, extensive organisation and significant financial resources. For young musicians, opportunities to perform as soloists with an orchestra are therefore rare.

Even highly talented graduates of prestigious music universities are not automatically given such opportunities. Many turn to international competitions, but even winning a competition may lead to only one orchestral performance, with no guarantee of the next. Recognising this challenge, Ms. Sonja Steindl founded IMK Vienna in 1992 with a clear mission: to create more high-quality and accessible orchestral performance opportunities across Europe.

Schwingungen at the Musikverein

As one of its signature projects, IMK launched the Schwingungen Concert Series at the Musikverein Golden Hall in Vienna in 2006, inviting three to four international orchestras each year. Over the past twenty years, IMK has successfully organised more than 58 concerts at the Golden Hall, with further performances already scheduled through 2028. Through this work IMK has developed unique expertise and extensive know-how in organising orchestral performances at this world-renowned venue.

From music education to the professional stage

Music education should no longer end in the classroom. It must evolve into a system that connects talented young musicians directly to real performance opportunities. Together with its partner universities, IMK is working to make this change a reality — building a bridge from music education to the professional stage. The IMK International Concerto Audition is the next step on that bridge.

Application Repertoire
Application guide

Video audition, open worldwide

The IMK International Concerto Audition is open to pianists, violinists and cellists. Applicants are selected through a video audition. Each applicant must submit an audition video of up to 20 minutes and a completed application form. The audition is evaluated by an international jury of conductors and distinguished instrumentalists.

I

Instruments

Piano · Violin · Cello. One soloist is selected for each instrument to form the trio for Beethoven's Triple Concerto.

II

Submission

An audition video of up to 20 minutes (Google Drive link) and the completed online application form, together with the €100 audition fee.

III

Jury

An international jury of conductors and distinguished instrumentalists evaluates all videos. Results are announced in the first week of December 2026.

General Provisions

The IMK International Concerto Audition is an international audition open to musicians of all nationalities. All judging is conducted by online video submission (Google Drive link). English is the official language of the audition.

Instruments & Repertoire

Piano · Violin · Cello. Repertoire requirements for each instrument are set out in detail on the Repertoire tab.

Age

There is no age limit. All applicants are judged by the same artistic standard.

Application

Applications are made through the official website. An application is valid only when the application form is completed and the audition fee of €100 is paid. The audition fee is non-refundable, except where the audition is cancelled by the organisers. Applications close on 30 November 2026, 23:59 CET.

Video Submission

  • The audition video may not exceed 20 minutes in total.
  • The performer's hands, instrument and face must be clearly visible throughout the performance.
  • The video must be a continuous, unedited recording of each submitted work. Only minor technical adjustments (e.g. trimming before and after the performance) are permitted.
  • Video and audio quality must be sufficient for the jury to clearly evaluate the performance.
  • Resolution: Full HD (1920 × 1080). 4K is not required. The video file must not exceed 2 GB in size (MP4 recommended).
  • Videos recorded on or after 1 January 2026 will be accepted.
  • The use of AI-generated performances, deepfakes, synthesised audio, or any technology that materially alters the performance is strictly prohibited.
  • The video must be uploaded to Google Drive and shared as a link set to "Anyone with the link can view". File name: Instrument_LastName_FirstName_Country.mp4

Judging

Performances are evaluated comprehensively on musicality, technique, interpretation, expression and overall artistry by an international jury of conductors and distinguished instrumentalists. The decisions of the jury are final. No appeals are accepted.

Selection & Concerts

Three soloists — one pianist, one violinist and one cellist — are selected. The selected musicians perform Beethoven's Triple Concerto in C major, Op. 56 with the North Czech Philharmonic Orchestra under Nayden Todorov at the New Year's Concert in Teplice (1 January 2027) and at the New Year's Concert in the Dvořák Hall of the Rudolfinum, Prague (3 January 2027). Details of rehearsals, travel and accommodation are communicated directly to the selected musicians.

Copyright & Personal Data

Applicants consent to the use of submitted videos and photographs for the promotion of the audition and the concerts. Personal data is used solely for the administration of the audition.

Disqualification

Submission of false information, violation of the video rules, misconduct, copyright infringement or violation of these regulations may result in disqualification without refund of the audition fee.

Agreement

By submitting an application, the applicant is deemed to have read and agreed to all regulations of the IMK International Concerto Audition.

Repertoire

Audition repertoire

The audition video may not exceed 20 minutes in total. Applicants perform one complete movement from a concerto of their own choice (free choice of repertoire). The movement must be performed in its entirety, from beginning to end, and recorded in one continuous take without any editing.

Piano

Maximum 20 minutes
  • One complete movement from a piano concerto of the applicant's choice, performed in its entirety from beginning to end.
  • Accompaniment: piano reduction (second piano) or orchestra.
  • When performing with piano accompaniment, standard cuts or reductions of orchestral tutti passages are permitted. No cuts to the solo part.
  • If the movement contains a cadenza, the cadenza must be included in the performance.

Violin

Maximum 20 minutes
  • One complete movement from a violin concerto of the applicant's choice, performed in its entirety without arbitrary cuts, omissions or edits to the solo part.
  • Accompaniment: piano reduction or orchestra.
  • When performing with piano accompaniment, standard cuts of orchestral tutti passages are permitted.
  • If the movement contains a cadenza, the cadenza must be included in the performance.

Cello

Maximum 20 minutes
  • One complete movement from a cello concerto of the applicant's choice, performed in its entirety without cuts to the solo part.
  • Accompaniment: piano reduction or orchestra.
  • When performing with piano accompaniment, standard cuts of orchestral tutti passages are permitted.
  • If the movement contains a cadenza, the cadenza must be included in the performance.

General notes

  • Works may be performed from memory or with the score.
  • Each work must be recorded as one continuous, unedited take.
  • Please state the composer, concerto and movement in the application form exactly as performed.
  • The jury reserves the right to disregard any portion of the video exceeding 20 minutes.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is there an age limit?
The audition is intended for young musicians at the start of their professional careers. Please contact us if you are unsure whether you are eligible.
What should I play in my audition video?
Repertoire is your choice. The jury is primarily looking for musicianship, technical command and stage presence. Including a passage from Beethoven's Triple Concerto is welcome but not required.
Can I apply as a pre-formed trio?
Applications are individual. Members of an existing trio are welcome to apply separately and may mention their ensemble in the biography; however, the jury selects each soloist independently.
Are travel and accommodation covered?
Details of travel, accommodation and rehearsal schedules will be communicated directly to the selected musicians after the announcement in December.
Is the audition fee refundable?
No. The €100 fee covers the administration of the audition and is non-refundable in all cases, including incomplete or withdrawn applications.
How will I know my application was received?
You will receive an automatic confirmation email after submitting the form. If you do not receive one within 24 hours, please check your spam folder and then contact us.
